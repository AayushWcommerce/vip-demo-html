      The Crafts Archive's Authenticity Promise

      We verify four things on every saree before it reaches you — fabric, yarn, silk, and zari — exactly as listed on this product's Authenticity Card.

      If an NABL-accredited lab report shows a mismatch on any of these four details within 45 days of delivery, we'll refund the full price of the saree plus your testing cost, up to ₹2,000.

      This promise applies to sarees, and covers fabric, yarn, silk, and zari composition only.

      Fine print: Fulfilled on The Crafts Archive's behalf by Wcommerce. To claim this promise, call the store's support number and return the saree along with your original purchase invoice and the NABL lab report within 45 days of delivery. The Crafts Archive reserves the right to have the item retested at another NABL-accredited lab before processing the refund.
