What the sarees page offers
All of these already exist on the live sarees page. The options come from the store's in-stock sarees, so a filter with nothing to offer is hidden.
  • Price (min – max)
  • Shop by craft
  • Occasion · Fabric · Colour
  • Search within sarees
  • Sort: Newest · Price low–high · Price high–low
Not on this page:
  • Size (never shown for sarees)
  • Discount, in-stock only, popular sort (the backend has no such option)
Tasks for the tester
  1. On the home page, tap the “Sarees under ₹1,999” banner.
  2. Check every saree costs ₹1,999 or less, and the price chip is selected.
  3. Pick a craft, then a fabric from Filters. Watch the link above the phone change.
  4. Sort by Price: low–high.
  5. Remove the price chip (✕) and see the dearer sarees come back.
  6. Copy the link above the phone into a new tab: it opens with the same filters.
Demo details
