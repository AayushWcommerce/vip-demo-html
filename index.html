6. Coupon — Training500

Partners who finish the training course get a ₹500 code. The same code does two different things depending on whether they have already paid — which is the part worth agreeing on before this ships.

If they have not paid yet
₹500 off the fee — they pay ₹999

The discount is applied when the payment order is created, so the partner is only ever charged ₹999. Nothing is paid out by us.

If they already paid ₹1499
₹500 credited to their wallet

There is nothing left to sell them, so the value goes to their Wcommerce wallet as withdrawable cash. Limited to once per partner, ever.

7. Custom Domain — locked vs unlocked

The Settings row a partner reaches Custom Domain through, in both states. The rows are deliberately identical apart from the lock — that is the whole gate, and it is the only thing a partner sees change when they buy.

The locked row is inert: it neither navigates nor opens payment. The lock is informational, and VIP is purchasable from exactly two places — the dashboard offer card and My Account. A partner who already has a domain set up is grandfathered: they keep it and see the unlocked row, whether or not they ever pay.
